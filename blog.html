THANKSGIVING

Psa 95:2-3 Let’s come before His presence with a song of thanksgiving, Let’s shout joyfully to Him in songs with instruments. 3. For the Lord is a great God And a great King above all gods

Too often being thankful may become feelings that gets buried under other things that are more pressing ...life happens to all of us. Being thankful is easy when surrounded by a crowd in the serenity of church or a praise service but when your car broke down ...the baby wakes up teething or your wife/husband walked out. But it is in the mess that we can lift our eyes to where our help comes from. Take a minute before the rush or just sitting with your loved ones to say thanks for the unexpected blessings or the answered prayers. Take a minute before you close your eyes to say to the Father ...Your Presence in my life is my greatest joy!! My salvation is my daily peace and Jesus my best friend. Wishing You and Yours lots of fun and great food!!
