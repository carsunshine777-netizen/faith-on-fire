The Spirit of Truth

He remains with you and will be in you.

“I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Helper, so that He may be with you forever; the Helper is the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it does not see Him or know Him; but you know Him because He remains with you and will be in you.”

— John 14:16-17

Who Is the Helper?

Jesus promised that we would never be left alone. The Spirit of truth is the third Person of the Trinity — the same Spirit who hovered over the waters at creation, who filled the prophets, and who now dwells in every believer. He is not a force or an idea. He is God Himself, present with us and in us forever.

The Light That Defies Darkness

In a world that often feels lost and broken, the Spirit of truth burns brightly through the people of God. He illuminates Scripture, convicts of sin, comforts the hurting, and empowers us to shine as witnesses. When we walk in step with Him, we become living flames that push back the darkness.

A Community on Fire

Faith on Fire exists so that believers can encourage one another to stay filled with the Spirit, rooted in the Word, and bold in shining light into places of darkness. Whether you are strong in faith or feeling broken, there is room here for the Spirit to do His work.
